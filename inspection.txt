## 4. CUSTOMER PURCHASE FLOW

Build on the existing NAVA authentication and payment system.

The intended customer flow is:

1. Customer signs in.
2. Customer chooses an available mobile plan and ZIP code.
3. NAVA displays plan features, price, applicable fees, and renewal terms.
4. Customer pays through the existing verified payment integration.
5. NAVA creates an order and records the payment result.
6. The order enters an explicit fulfillment status.
7. Once the provider confirms successful activation, NAVA records the assigned number, carrier, plan, activation date, expiry date, and renewal price.
8. The customer sees the line and its status under an appropriate account section, such as My Mobile Lines.

Use explicit statuses, for example:

* Pending payment.
* Payment received.
* Awaiting fulfillment.
* Processing.
* Active.
* Renewal pending.
* Renewed.
* Failed or refund pending.

A successful payment is NOT proof that the provider has activated a line.

Do not promise instant activation unless the supplier integration proves it. Do not fabricate phone numbers, activation results, delivery receipts, or provider responses.

Where no authorized provider API is available, design a controlled manual-fulfillment process with an appropriate admin view. Do not automate or scrape an unauthorized consumer checkout.

## 5. CUSTOMER RENEWALS

Every active line must have an expiry date and a renewal price.

Implement the necessary application and database support for:

* Showing the line's current status, carrier, plan, number, expiry date, and renewal option.
* Letting the customer request a renewal and pay NAVA.
* Recording the payment and creating a renewal request.
* Processing the renewal through an authorized provider integration or a transparent manual admin workflow.
* Updating the expiry date only after renewal is confirmed successful.
* Recording renewal history, provider results, payment references, and failures.
* Preventing duplicate renewal requests and duplicate charges.
* Providing a clear processing status to the customer.

Do not assume provider renewal is instant. Do not generate a new eSIM QR code for an ordinary plan renewal unless the provider actually requires one.

Investigate the fact that the provider's retail website also offers direct renewals. Do not claim NAVA can prevent customers from renewing directly with the carrier or provider. Flag any dependence on dealer-only renewal capabilities that have not been verified.
