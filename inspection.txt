## 6. BUILD A PROPER RECHARGE EXPERIENCE

Create a dedicated Recharge page on the NAVA site, accessible from both the navigation and My eSIM Lines.

The flow should resemble the Nexitel recharge experience I showed you.

1. The customer opens Recharge.
2. They enter their US phone number.
3. NAVA verifies that the number belongs to a line purchased through NAVA and that the customer is authorized to access it.
4. The page displays the actual line, plan name, service status, expiry date, remaining time when it can be determined reliably, and the renewal price.
5. The customer reviews the itemized total and confirms the recharge.
6. Payment is processed once.
7. The renewal and new expiry date are recorded correctly and displayed to the customer.

For active lines, an additional 30 days should normally be added after the current expiry rather than replacing the remaining days, subject to the actual provider's rules.

A phone number alone must not reveal private customer details or allow someone to renew or control another person's line. Protect the lookup appropriately.

The recharge page and the Renew button on My eSIM Lines must share consistent payment and renewal logic. Do not create two independent charging mechanisms.
